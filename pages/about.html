About NeuralPath & Sources

Project Overview

NeuralPath is built as an interactive AI learning portal tailored for high school students (grades 9–12), developed for the TSA Webmaster competitive event. It demystifies artificial intelligence, teaches practical prompt literacy, and reinforces academic integrity.

Citations & References
